I would use a Hybrid approach for the hotel booking system.
For critical areas such as payments, reservation consistency, 
and security, I would use a Waterfall-oriented approach so that requirements,
risks, and test scenarios are clearly defined before release.
This can reduce the chance of serious production issues and help
prevent customer dissatisfaction. After the core system is stable, 
I would use Agile iterations to improve the product based on user feedback and new feature requests.
